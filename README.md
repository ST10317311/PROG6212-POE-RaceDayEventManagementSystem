Part 1

Brief description of the system:
The RaceDay event management system is an app/website that tracks a participant's speed, position, and the events they have attended.

The Participant role can create a personal account on the platform, access to viewing active events, enter themselves into events, and track their personal results (how many km per hour have they been moving on the road, and what position they were in at each checkpoint). 

The Organiser role can create, edit, and delete events, manage event categories, capture participant results, and view all event enrolments.
